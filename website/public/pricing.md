# Pricing — Grit

## Open Source
- Price: $0 (Free Forever)
- Features:
  - Local SQLite State Management
  - CommitScribe AI Integration (BYO Key)
  - Intelligence Dashboard
  - Historical Redistributor (Spread)
  - Self-Healing Sync Engine
  - Loguru-based Professional Logging
- Platform Support: macOS, Linux, Windows (via Python/uv)

Grit is 100% open-source and respects your privacy. All state is stored locally on your machine in `~/.grit/`.
